# Warranty Information

All electronics purchased come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering
defects in materials and workmanship. This warranty does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

Extended warranty plans (2-year and 3-year) can be purchased at checkout or
within 30 days of the original purchase, and cover one accidental-damage claim
per year in addition to the manufacturer's defect coverage.

To file a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a
description of the issue; most claims are resolved within 3 business days.
