# Return Policy

Customers may return unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened electronics can be returned within 15 days if all original accessories and
packaging are included; a 10% restocking fee applies to opened electronics.

Final-sale items (marked "Clearance" or "Final Sale" on the product page) cannot
be returned or exchanged under any circumstances.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of
the warehouse receiving the returned item.
