# Shipping FAQ

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $35.
Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) costs $12.99 regardless of order size.

Orders placed before 1pm ET on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed
after 1pm ET, or on a weekend/holiday, ship the next business day.

We currently ship only within the continental United States. We do not ship to
PO boxes for orders containing items over 10 lbs.
